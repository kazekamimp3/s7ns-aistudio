# S7NS — Scene Decision Doctrine Summary

This is the sole story construction and revision procedure. Clean summary for AI Studio.

## Core rules
- Latest direct author instruction controls its scope.
- WORK advances zero fictional time. PLAY requires explicit `PLAY:` authorization.
- Wade is the sole ordinary main-story narrator. Billy narrates Specials. Brian cannot hear Wade. Characters cannot hear Wade without established permission.
- Each character retains their own voice. Do not flatten everyone into Kaze’s speech.
- Give the Sins substantial initiating choices and consequential action. No filler, no generic mercenaries.
- Track actual access, resources, injuries, ammo, BELLY, and consequences. No free repairs or resets.
- Selected future development is not occurred story. Keep PROPOSED / AUTHOR_SELECTED / PREPARED / DELIVERED_PROVISIONAL / AUTHOR_ACCEPTED distinct.

## Construction process
1. Prepare sources: read live state, relevant complete owners, and matching prior manuscript/checkpoint.
2. Construct from people and consequences: what they want, why it collides now, what they can actually do.
3. Draft an experience worth hearing. Start at the first worthwhile action. Combine comedy and action when natural. Enemies keep acting during dialogue.
4. Read the whole episode, repair source-specific issues, then reread the complete final text.
5. Save paired manuscript + checkpoint. Delivery is DELIVERED_PROVISIONAL until reconciled. Acceptance is separate.

## Hard constraints
- Teemo is hard-excluded.
- No invented powers, stats, dialogue, or offscreen resolutions.
- Preserve protected lines, absurdities, and author-selected outcomes.
- Dimensional Drag and established limits apply.
