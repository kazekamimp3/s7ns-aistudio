## 3. Dutch's intentional hypocrisy callback

The two conversations must remain separate and preserve their deliberate contrast:

**Earlier:** Brian wants revenge against the Sins. Dutch tells him, “Revenge is a fool's game.”

**Later:** Dutch lists his own grievances against Brian, including money, Tahiti, and Brian's betrayal of the gang, then begs Dr. Evil to pursue revenge against Brian.

The later request exposes Dutch's hypocrisy. He condemns revenge when Brian wants it, then seeks revenge when he feels personally wronged. Kaze explicitly confirmed this is character-faithful Dutch behavior.

Do not remove, soften, explain away, or accidentally resolve the contradiction. Do not make Dutch conveniently acknowledge his hypocrisy unless Kaze directs it.
